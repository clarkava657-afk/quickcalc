Percentage Calculator

The Percentage Calculator makes quick work of percentage math, whether you're working out a discount, a tip, exam scores, or how much something has increased or decreased. Just enter your numbers and get an instant, accurate result.

This tool is handy for everyday situations like shopping sales, splitting bills, tracking savings goals, or checking grades, without needing to do the math by hand.

How it works

Depending on what you're calculating, the tool applies the right percentage formula automatically, for example, dividing one number by another and multiplying by 100 to find what percentage one value is of another, or applying a percentage to a number directly. You simply enter your values and QuickCalc handles the calculation.

Word Count Tool

The Word Count Tool instantly counts the number of words, characters, and sentences in any text you paste or type in. It's a simple but useful tool for writers, students, and anyone working with word or character limits.

Common uses include checking essay or assignment word counts, staying within social media character limits, or keeping cover letters and resumes concise.

How it works

As you type or paste text into the box, the tool scans the content in real time, counting spaces to determine word breaks and tallying total characters and sentences, then displaying the results instantly on screen.
