Age Calculator

The Age Calculator helps you find out exactly how old you are, or how old someone else is, based on a date of birth. Simply enter the birth date, and the tool instantly calculates the full age in years, months, and days.

This is useful for filling out official forms, checking eligibility for age-restricted activities, planning birthdays, or just satisfying curiosity about exact age down to the day.

How it works

The calculator takes the date you enter and compares it to today's date. It then works out the difference, accounting for varying month lengths and leap years, to give you a precise and accurate result every time.

BMI Calculator

The BMI (Body Mass Index) Calculator helps you estimate whether your weight is in a healthy range for your height. You enter your weight and height, and the tool calculates your BMI score along with the category it falls into (underweight, normal, overweight, or obese).

BMI is a widely used screening tool by health professionals worldwide, though it's important to remember it's a general guide and not a complete diagnosis of health.

How it works

BMI is calculated using the formula: weight (kg) divided by height (m) squared. QuickCalc handles this calculation instantly once you enter your details, so you don't have to do any manual math.
